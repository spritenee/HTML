Today's phrase you can use · 今天一句能用的话

你 好

Nǐ hǎo · Hello

Listen first. Understand more. You do not have to catch every word — and you do not have to write this character yet. Saying it is enough for today.

01 Listen — 先听一遍 · 02 Notice — 注意声调 · 03 Say it — 跟着说一遍
