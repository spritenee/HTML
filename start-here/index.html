Start Here

Start Here

Pick the person you are. Each way in is three small steps — no characters first, no test, no rush.

Way 1 — I am visiting China for the first time

  1. Read one culture piece — food, festivals, or everyday life, in plain English.
  2. Learn one phrase you can use — greeting, thanking, ordering food. Listen first, say it, use it. A new one is on the homepage.
  3. Read one news piece — so the place you are visiting makes a little more sense when you arrive.

Then, if you like: Visit China with Allen.

Way 2 — I love Chinese culture, but I do not speak Chinese

  1. Start with culture: one idea at a time, English first, Chinese second.
  2. Follow the news: what happened, why it matters in China, three words to learn.
  3. Try one Tai Chi movement: culture you can feel in your body, not only read about.

Way 3 — I want to learn Chinese, from real life

  1. One useful phrase a day — from a real scene, not from a character list. Listen, notice the tones, say it.
  2. Learn why it works that way — the culture behind the sentence, in one short paragraph.
  3. Use it once, somewhere real: in a shop, in a message, or with Allen.

Characters come later, as a footnote — they are not lesson one.

Way 4 — I want to try Tai Chi

  1. Read how a lesson works: stand, breathe, move — one movement at a time.
  2. Practise Qi Shi for ten minutes a day, until it feels natural.
  3. Then the next movement. There is no hurry, and no exam at the end.

After your first three steps

You should be able to greet someone, order a simple meal, understand one piece of Chinese culture a little better, and move through one Tai Chi opening — not fluently, but confidently enough to keep going.

Next step: Contact Allen and tell him which way in you picked, and what you would like next.
