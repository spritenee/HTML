About Allen

About Allen

Allen lives in China, and this site is his window into it — for people outside China who want to understand it, in plain English, at a slow pace.

He is not a school, and this is not a course you have to finish. He is one person you can ask: about Chinese culture, about a news story, about a sentence you want to say, about a Tai Chi movement, or about visiting China with a local friend.

His approach is simple: start with a real scene, give you a real sentence or a real story, explain why Chinese people say it or do it that way, and then let you try it. Culture is not an extra chapter at the end — it is the reason the sentence sounds the way it does. Tai Chi follows the same idea: one movement at a time, slowly, until it feels natural.

Full introduction, teaching background, and a longer bilingual introduction will be added here. This page is intentionally short at launch, rather than filled with claims that are not yet written. Tai Chi style and form, and the city and range for local guiding, will also be added here once Allen confirms them.

What you can do here

  • Understand China, in small pieces: food, festivals, family, everyday life, and history — English first, Chinese second
  • Learn Chinese you can actually use, from real scenes — not from a character list
  • Learn Tai Chi, one movement at a time — and the Chinese words that go with it
  • Read China news, explained in plain English, with the words you need to follow it
  • Visit China with a local friend, when you are ready
