China News, Explained

News about China, explained in plain English — so you can understand not only what happened, but why it matters there.

How each interpretation works

Every piece follows the same four parts, so you always know where you are:

  1. What happened — the facts, in three or four short sentences. No jargon first.
  2. Why it matters in China — the cultural, social, or historical background a foreign reader usually misses.
  3. Words to learn — three Chinese words from the story, with characters, pinyin, and a plain English meaning, so the news becomes a language lesson too.
  4. What to watch next — one question to keep in mind, not a prediction.

What this section is not

It is not a news feed, and it does not copy whole articles. Each piece is Allen's own explanation, in his own words, with sources named. It does not take a political side for you — it explains the background so you can form your own view.

First interpretations

The first interpretations will be linked here as they are published. Nothing here is a placeholder article — if a title is not linked yet, it is still being written.

Each published piece also gets a spoken version: Allen reads “What happened” and “Why it matters in China” aloud, 1–2 minutes, so you can listen as well as read.
